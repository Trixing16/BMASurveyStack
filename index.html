Photogrammetry Settings
Review the settings of a completed mission. Upload or paste your project file to instantly extract overlap percentages, GSD, and intrinsic camera values so you can verify what was actually flown.
In Dev
📡
Processing PPK Positions
End-to-end PPK position processing tool. Takes raw base and rover RINEX, runs differential correction, applies antenna offsets, and outputs camera event positions in survey datum ready for photogrammetry.
In Dev
🛰️
DJI MRK Parser
Parses DJI M350 / M4D .MRK PPK output files and transforms event coordinates from WGS84 to AMG66/MGA. Validates against base RINEX, flags quality outliers, and exports corrected camera positions for photogrammetry ingestion.
In Dev
📉
Loss & Dilution Visualisation
Interactive visualisation of mining loss and dilution across blast blocks. Overlays design vs actual dig boundaries, quantifies ore lost to waste and waste diluting ore, and flags problem areas for reconciliation review.
In Dev
📐
Bund Heights & Grade Check
Field-ready checker for bund height compliance and haul road cross-fall. Calculates design vs as-surveyed grades, flags out-of-tolerance sections, and generates a formatted daily report for site sign-off.
